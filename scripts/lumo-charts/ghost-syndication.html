A job on three boards is not three jobs

One real job, posted on three sites. The first scorer could give it 60 to 90 points from signals that all measured the same harmless thing.

SignalHow the first scorer read itThe rule now
Multiple IDs Fired on almost any job found on two sites, because each board gives the job its own ID. Counts only when the IDs come from the same site.
Frequent reposting Read the different dates on different boards as reposts. Counts only on the same source. Its maximum dropped from 35 points to 25.
Conflicting dates Counted that same date difference a second time, at a gap of 7 days. Needs a gap of more than 21 days, and cannot fire when reposting already did.
Missing data No visible date, or little text read from the page, added points. Shown for information. Adds nothing to the score.
High confidenceneeds one of five signals with real evidence: closed in the applicant system, a broken apply link, frequent reposting, duplicate IDs, or reported layoffs.
